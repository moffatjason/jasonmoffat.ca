PowerPlay

Timeplay2014 to 2019Themed triviaBig screen and controller
PowerPlay on the theatre screen.

PowerPlay was Timeplay's themed trivia format for the pre-show: the audience answers on their phones, the big screen keeps score, and the theme changes per campaign, from sci-fi movie rounds to a sponsor's brand.

The stills are themed rounds and screens from the leaderboard, buzz-in, order-up, pick-three, reveal-it, and head-to-head modes.
