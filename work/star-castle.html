Star Castle

Carleton University, Algonquin College, and personal workInteractive Multimedia and Design, 3D Modelling20093D model

A castle model for a 3D modelling course. The brief was to design and model a castle in a chosen style; this one went futuristic, inspired by the band Starcastle and the artwork of their 1976 self-titled album.
