Dhalsim

Carleton University, Algonquin College, and personal workInteractive Multimedia and Design, 3D Modelling20103D character

A character model for a 3D modelling course. The brief was to model a character of choice with specific parts of the body in detail, then texture and rig it for animation.

Picked Street Fighter's Dhalsim for his expressive face and a muscular build that made the body straightforward. The focus was the face, ears, and hands.
