Games for cinema audiences

Timeplay2014 to 2019Lead game developer

Timeplay ran a platform in Cineplex theatres where the audience plays on their phones, in sync with the big screen, before the movie starts. Led a small team building the games, both branded titles for advertisers on fixed campaign deadlines and the platform's own trivia and party formats.

Each entry is a short capture of the game running on the theatre screen. "Big screen and controller" means the audience's phones drove the game; "big screen" titles played out on the screen alone.
