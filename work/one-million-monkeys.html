One Million Monkeys

BlackCherry Digital MediaBeeline Interactive20122D text-based mobile game

Million Monkeys is about building silly stories with friends: collaborate a few words at a time, overcome tricky rules, and earn bananas for satisfying a set of brain-bending criteria. Bananas raise your rank, add stickers to your stories, and lift limitations.

BlackCherry built the game with Beeline Interactive through a four-month service contract in 2012, responsible for all of production from art direction through programming, on Beeline's concept. It shipped free-to-play on iOS and Android.

Worked mainly on the social layer, letting players invite friends and share stories through Facebook, Game Center, Twitter, email, or the device's contacts. Built several custom menu tools on NGUI in Unity, and spent a large share of the schedule debugging before and after launch and implementing feature requests.
