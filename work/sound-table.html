Sound Table

Carleton University, Algonquin College, and personal workPersonal2013Music setup

Music has been a constant alongside games. Sound Table was the first finished track from a setup built around Ableton Live 9 with Operator, a Novation Launchpad, and a rack of Korg pads (the nanoSeries 2, KaossPad 3, KaossPad Quad, Kaossilator Pro, and the minis).

The chain runs the Korg pads through a TC Electronic Impact Twin into Ableton and onto the Launchpad, which controls how each looped clip launches, with per-track volume and pan on the nanoKontrol.
