Wicka Wicka

Carleton University, Algonquin College, and personal workAlgonquin College design studio2010Multimedia project

A group project from a design studio at Algonquin College: a collapsible multimedia touch table built around sound synthesis. Tracking software reads symbols on the underside of physical markers and displays musical instruments on the table surface.

Multiple fingers can play pianos, scratch records, and adjust modulators. MIDI is sent on to studio production software and visualizers. Built with Processing and OpenGL, with FL Studio 8 on the sound side.
