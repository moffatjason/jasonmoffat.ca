Robot Battle

Carleton University, Algonquin College, and personal workCarleton University, IMD3900 3D Modelling20103D animation
The short.
Animation playblast with the rigs still in progress, showing the IK setup in grey, rig handles in blue, and spline handles in red.

A character-driven animated short made for a 3D modelling course, inspired by early mecha series like Gundam Wing and Neon Genesis Evangelion. Since the project ran over one semester, robotic characters were a deliberate choice: easier to animate than anything organic. Once animation started, simplified the camera angles and environment to keep the focus on movement.

The rigs were the interesting part: simple but mechanically accurate. The arms and legs use inverse kinematics with extra handles for parts like the shoulder plates; the monster's tail is a spline with several handles for full control over curve and twist.
